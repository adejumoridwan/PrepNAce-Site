## 1. Introduction

Welcome to PrepNAce!

At PrepNAce, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information of our users.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, process, share and protect personal information when you access or use our mobile application, website and associated educational services.

PrepNAce is an independent educational platform designed to help students prepare for examinations through practice questions, mock examinations, learning resources, performance tracking and other educational tools.

By using PrepNAce, you acknowledge that you have been given an opportunity to read this Privacy Policy.

We encourage students, parents and guardians to read this document carefully to understand how personal information is handled.

We are committed to handling personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and relevant directives issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use PrepNAce, we may collect the following categories of information.

### 2.1 Account and Registration Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

- Full name or display name.
- Email address.
- Phone number.
- Username.
- Account identification information.
- Login and authentication information.

This information helps us create and manage your account, authenticate your identity and provide access to our services.

### 2.2 Student Profile and Academic Information

To provide relevant examination preparation services, we may collect information such as:

- Educational level.
- Examination category (JAMB, WAEC, NECO or POST-UTME).
- Examination year.
- Selected subjects.
- Preferred university or institution.
- State of residence or institution, where provided.
- Previous JAMB scores or other academic scores, where voluntarily provided.
- Intended course of study, where provided.
- Other educational preferences.

We use this information to personalise learning experiences, recommend relevant practice materials and provide educational insights.

Providing optional academic information is voluntary unless a particular feature requires it.

### 2.3 Learning Activities and Performance Information

When you use PrepNAce, we may collect and process information about your learning activities, including:

- Questions attempted.
- Selected answers.
- Correct and incorrect responses.
- Examination and mock test scores.
- Time spent on practice activities.
- Completed examinations.
- Subject and topic performance.
- Learning progress.
- XP points and achievements.
- Daily streaks and badges.
- Leaderboard rankings.
- Study activity and related learning statistics.

We use this information to calculate performance metrics, maintain learning records, provide progress reports and improve your examination preparation experience.

### 2.4 Device and Technical Information

To operate and protect the platform, we may collect certain technical information, including:

- Device type and model.
- Operating system and version.
- Application version.
- Device identifiers or installation identifiers.
- Device registration and licence activation information.
- IP address, where available.
- Network and connection information.
- Crash reports and technical diagnostic information.

This information helps us troubleshoot technical problems, protect accounts, enforce licence restrictions and maintain platform security.

**Device Registration and Licence Information**

PrepNAce uses device registration information to associate purchased licence keys with the devices on which they are activated.

We may retain information relating to device activation, licence status and registration history to manage access to paid services and prevent unauthorised reuse of licence keys.

If a user performs a factory reset, reinstalls the operating system or otherwise removes the device's activation information, a new licence key may be required to activate PrepNAce again.

Our handling of device registration information is subject to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.

### 2.5 Payment and Transaction Information

When you purchase a PrepNAce licence or other paid service, we may collect or receive information such as:

- Transaction reference.
- Payment amount.
- Date and time of payment.
- Payment status.
- Purchased package or licence category.
- Licence activation status.
- Relevant payment confirmation information.

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers, including Paystack where applicable.

**PrepNAce does not intend to collect or store your complete debit card details, card PIN or banking passwords.** Such sensitive payment credentials should be entered only through the authorised payment provider's secure payment interface.

Payment providers process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security procedures.

### 2.6 Communication and Support Information

When you contact us for assistance, submit feedback, report an error or make a complaint, we may collect:

- Your name.
- Email address.
- Phone number.
- Description of your complaint or enquiry.
- Relevant transaction or account information.
- Other information you voluntarily provide.

We use this information to respond to enquiries, investigate complaints and improve our services.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing Personal Information

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, personal information must be processed on an appropriate lawful basis.

Depending on the specific activity, PrepNAce may rely on the following lawful bases:

**4.1 Performance of a Contract**

We process information where it is necessary to create and manage your account, provide purchased services, activate licences and fulfil our obligations to users.

**4.2 Consent**

We may rely on your consent when processing information for activities that require consent, such as certain optional data collection, marketing communications or non-essential analytics.

You may withdraw consent where consent is the applicable lawful basis.

**4.3 Legitimate Interests**

We may process certain information where necessary for legitimate business interests, provided those interests do not override your fundamental rights and freedoms.

These interests may include platform security, fraud prevention, service improvement and protection of our intellectual property.

**4.4 Legal Obligations**

We may process or retain certain information where required to comply with applicable laws, regulatory requirements, financial recordkeeping obligations or lawful requests from competent authorities.

We will not rely on one lawful basis indiscriminately for every processing activity.

## 7. Children's Privacy

PrepNAce is an educational platform that may be used by secondary school students and other examination candidates, including individuals below the age of 18.

We recognise that children require additional protection when their personal information is processed.

Where a user is a child or otherwise lacks legal capacity to provide valid consent, we will obtain parental or legal guardian consent where required under applicable law.

We may also take appropriate steps to verify age and parental or guardian consent where necessary.

We aim to:

1. Collect only information reasonably necessary to provide educational services.
2. Avoid unnecessary collection of sensitive personal information from children.
3. Use children's information primarily for educational, account management and security purposes.
4. Apply appropriate safeguards to protect children's personal information.
5. Provide appropriate mechanisms for parents or guardians to make privacy-related enquiries and requests.

Parents and legal guardians are encouraged to supervise their children's use of PrepNAce.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information without the required consent, please contact us.

We will investigate the matter and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law.

When personal information is no longer necessary, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or anonymise it securely, subject to applicable legal obligations.

## 12. Your Data Protection Rights

Under applicable Nigerian data protection laws, you have rights regarding your personal information.

These rights may include:

### 12.1 Right to Be Informed

You have the right to know how your personal information is collected, used, stored and shared.

### 12.2 Right of Access

You may request confirmation of whether we process your personal information and request access to relevant information.

### 12.3 Right to Rectification

You may request correction of inaccurate, incomplete or outdated personal information.

### 12.4 Right to Erasure

You may request deletion of your personal information where the applicable legal requirements are satisfied.

### 12.5 Right to Restrict Processing

You may request that we restrict certain processing activities where permitted by law.

### 12.6 Right to Object

You may object to certain processing activities, including processing based on legitimate interests and direct marketing, subject to applicable legal conditions.

### 12.7 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where we rely on consent as the lawful basis for processing, you may withdraw that consent.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

### 12.8 Right to Data Portability

Where applicable, you may request a copy of certain personal information in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.

### 12.9 Right to Complain

If you believe your personal information has been handled unlawfully, you may submit a complaint to us or lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To exercise your rights, please contact us using the details provided in Section 17.

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

Some requests may be subject to lawful exceptions, including obligations to retain financial records or information required for legal claims.

## 13. Account Deletion and Personal Information Removal

You may request the deletion of your PrepNAce account and associated personal information.

To make a deletion request, contact us through our official support email.

When we receive a valid request, we will:

1. Verify that the request comes from the account holder or an authorised representative.
2. Review the information associated with the account.
3. Delete or anonymise personal information where legally permissible and technically practicable.
4. Retain information where required by applicable laws or necessary for legitimate legal purposes.
5. Inform you of the outcome, subject to applicable law.

Deleting your account may result in the loss of access to:

- Learning history.
- Examination attempts.
- Performance statistics.
- XP points and achievements.
- Account preferences.
- Other account-associated information.

Deletion of your account does not automatically entitle you to a refund for previously purchased licences. Refund requests are governed by our separate Refund Policy.

Certain transaction, payment or licensing records may be retained after account deletion where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
