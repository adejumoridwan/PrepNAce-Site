Working through a full exam paper can be useful, but it may not show you exactly which part of a subject needs more attention. Topic-based practice gives revision a sharper focus.

## Find the gap behind a wrong answer

When you miss a question, identify the idea or step that caused difficulty. Was the concept unfamiliar, did you misread the question, or did a calculation go wrong? The answer guides what to revise next.

## Practise, review, then return

Choose a small group of questions from one topic. Review your answers and explanations, revisit the learning material, then try a fresh set later. Returning to a topic helps check whether the understanding has stuck.

## Use full tests to check readiness

Once you have worked through the important topics, timed practice can help you get comfortable with exam pacing and switching between question types.

A strong preparation plan uses both focused practice and full assessments. The balance can shift as the examination gets closer.
