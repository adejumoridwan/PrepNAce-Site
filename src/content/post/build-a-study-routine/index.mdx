A useful study routine is not the most crowded timetable. It is a plan you can return to consistently, even when a week becomes busy.

## Start with a short weekly review

Write down your subjects, upcoming tests and topics that feel difficult. Choose a few priorities for the week instead of trying to revise everything at once.

## Make each session specific

Replace a broad task such as “study Biology” with an action you can finish: review one topic, answer a set of questions, or explain a concept from memory. Clear tasks make it easier to begin and to notice when you are done.

## Leave room for practice and rest

Alternate focused review with question practice. Short breaks help you reset, and a little flexibility makes a routine more sustainable than a tightly packed plan.

## Review and adjust

At the end of the week, note what worked and where you need more time. A study plan is a tool, not a test of discipline. Change it as your needs change.

Start with a manageable routine, then build from what you can keep doing.
