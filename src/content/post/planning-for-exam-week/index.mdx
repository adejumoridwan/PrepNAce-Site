Exam week can feel overwhelming when every subject competes for attention. A simple plan can help you use your time deliberately and protect space for rest.

## Map the week first

List your exam dates and the topics you still want to review. Give earlier exams priority, while reserving brief review sessions for subjects scheduled later.

## Choose high-value review tasks

Focus on important concepts, questions you have previously found difficult, and materials your teacher has emphasized. Avoid spending the entire day rereading notes without checking what you can recall.

## Prepare practical details early

Check the time and location of each exam and prepare any permitted materials the day before. This keeps last-minute logistics from taking attention away from rest.

## Keep the basics in view

Take breaks, eat regularly and aim for enough sleep. Rest is part of preparation because it supports concentration and recall.

A good exam-week plan is realistic, flexible and focused on the next useful step.
